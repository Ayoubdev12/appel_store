# Privacy Policy for YT video

**Last updated: April 29, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how YT video ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Data Collection

We do **not** require account registration and we do **not** directly collect personal information like your name, email address, or phone number.

Depending on how you use the App, limited technical data may be processed by third-party SDKs and service providers, such as:

- Device information (device model, OS version, app version, language).
- Network information (IP address, connection type).
- App and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, ad load/show events).
- Device identifiers (such as advertising identifiers, when available and permitted).
- Diagnostic data (crash/performance data generated by third-party SDKs, if any).

## 2. Third-Party Services and SDKs

The App uses third-party SDKs and network services for core functionality (video/media handling, downloading, ads, and notifications). These providers may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Main third-party services include:

- **Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob + UMP consent)**  
  Used to request, load, and display ads, and to manage ad consent flows.  
  Privacy Policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

- **Yandex Mobile Ads SDK**  
  Used as an alternative ads provider depending on remote configuration.  
  Privacy Policy: [https://yandex.com/legal/mobile_ads_sdk_privacy/](https://yandex.com/legal/mobile_ads_sdk_privacy/)

- **App Tracking Transparency (iOS)**  
  Used to request tracking permission on iOS before personalized tracking-related ads behavior where applicable.  
  Apple Privacy: [https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

- **Video/Media and Networking Libraries** (for playback, content retrieval, and downloads)  
  These may connect to content sources and process standard network metadata (such as IP address and request headers) required for streaming/downloading.

## 3. Ads Integration

The App may show ads (including app-open and interstitial ads). Ads are delivered by configured ad networks (Google AdMob and/or Yandex Ads).

What this means for users:

- Ads may use device/network information to serve and measure ads.
- On iOS, tracking authorization is requested through Apple's ATT prompt when required.
- If tracking is denied, ads may still be shown but may be less personalized.
- We do not sell your personal data ourselves; ad partners may process technical identifiers under their own policies.

## 4. Permissions We Request

The App may request the following permissions, depending on platform and features used:

- **Internet**: required to load video/media content, remote app configuration, and ads.
- **Storage / Media access (Android)**: required for saving downloaded media and reading user media files when needed for playback.
- **Notifications (Android 13+)**: required for media playback notifications and playback controls.
- **Foreground service / wake lock (Android)**: required to keep audio playback and media tasks running in the background.
- **Tracking (iOS ATT prompt)**: requested to support personalized advertising where applicable.
- **Microphone (iOS string present)**: requested only if a feature needing microphone access is used.

Permissions can be managed any time in your device settings.

## 5. How Data Is Used

Data processed in or through the App is used to:

- Provide core app features (video playback, audio playback, and downloads).
- Deliver ads and prevent ad abuse/fraud.
- Store app preferences locally on device.
- Maintain app stability, troubleshoot issues, and improve user experience.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not share personal data with third parties for our own independent marketing.

However, technical data may be shared with:

- Ad providers and their measurement partners (for ad delivery, frequency control, and reporting).
- Service providers needed to operate app features (network/CDN/content endpoints).
- Authorities, if required by applicable law.

## 7. User Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

- Request access to personal data processed about you.
- Request deletion of personal data, when applicable.
- Withdraw consent (for example, ad tracking permissions) at any time.
- Object to or restrict certain processing in some cases.

You can also reset advertising identifiers or limit ad tracking in device settings.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the email below.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is **not directed to children under 13** (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.
